NSSTA-101 • Core SSS / ISS

Advanced Multi-Stage Sampling & Survey Methodology

Stratified sampling, cluster designs, variance estimation, and non-sampling error minimization in large-scale socio-economic surveys.

⏱️ 8 Weeks • 40 Credits
NSSTA-204 • Macroeconomic Analytics

National Accounts & Macro Deflator Analytics

SNA 2008 frameworks, Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP) estimation, Supply and Use Tables (SUT), and inflation deflator modeling.

⏱️ 6 Weeks • 30 Credits

Public Data Science & Statistical Computing in Python

Pandas, NumPy, automated data validation pipelines, statistical imputation methods, and DPDP-compliant data masking.

⏱️ 10 Weeks • 50 Credits
NSSTA-412 • Field Protocol

Tablet-based enumerator administration, biometric geolocation verification, quality audits, and field supervisor escalation procedures.

⏱️ 4 Weeks • 20 Credits

Trainee Elo Rating System

A live, mathematical score reflecting your overall knowledge, problem-solving speed, and accuracy across assessments.

What Does "Elo" Mean? (Full Form & Origin)

Origin & Creator: Named after Dr. Arpad Elo, a physicist and mathematician who created the universal standard for measuring skill levels.

Full Form in Learning: In educational and digital training platforms, Elo stands for Expected Learning Outcome (or Relative Competency Rating).

How It Works in Practice: Just like a chess rating or sports league score, your Elo rating starts at 1400. When you perform well on tough assessments, your score climbs. Consistent accuracy and quick responses award additional bonus points.

National Percentile

Your percentile compares your performance against all candidates across the country. An 88.4th percentile means your score is higher than 88.4% of all active test-takers.

Competency Level & Tier

The standardized qualification tier assigned to you based on verified assessment milestones.

Tier Classifications
  • Tier 1 — Foundational: Basic conceptual familiarity and introductory coursework.
  • Tier 2 — Intermediate: Consistent scores on standard evaluation modules.
  • Tier 3 — Proficient: High accuracy across advanced multi-step problem solving.
  • Tier 4 — Advanced Master: Top 5% ranking with exceptional speed and zero proctoring violations.
Total Speed Bonus Banked

Extra score points awarded for rapid, decisive, and accurate answers in timed tests.

How Speed Points Are Calculated

Every question in a timed test has a countdown timer (e.g., 45 or 60 seconds). When you select the correct answer before the timer expires, each remaining second is converted into 1 Speed Bonus Point!

Speed Points Earned = Remaining Clock Seconds (On Correct Answers Only)

Note: Incorrect answers or expired timers award 0 speed points.

Proctored Integrity Index

Tracks strict adherence to ethical examination standards and anti-cheating regulations.

Anti-Cheating Watchdog Rules

During an active assessment, the automated proctor monitors the browser window:

  • Tab Switching: Opening another tab or minimizing the browser triggers an instant violation.
  • Window Focus: Clicking outside the assessment screen pauses and flags the session.
  • Developer Tools: Pressing F12 or inspection shortcuts immediately terminates the exam with a zero score.

A score of 100% (Merit) confirms flawless ethical compliance throughout all examinations.

Active Skill Gaps

Tracks specific syllabus topic areas where questions were answered incorrectly or where time expired during evaluations.

How It Works

Whenever an assessment is submitted, the diagnostic engine intercepts missed questions, identifies the underlying core concept, and logs it here as an active deficit requiring study and review.

Action to take: Click Why? on any card to inspect the exact mistakes, or click Suggest Preparation for an AI study plan.
Cleared & Mastered Topics

Weak areas you have studied, practiced, and successfully solved through a retest.

How It Works

After reviewing the study guide, click Retest Topic on any card. If you score at least 66% (2 out of 3 questions correct), the weak area is marked as Mastered, and the card turns green.

Reward: Each cleared topic immediately credits +50 bonus Elo points to your score.
Overall Readiness Index

A simple percentage score showing how much of the syllabus you have fully mastered.

Calculation Formula
Readiness Score = (Cleared Topics / Total Tracked Topics) x 100%

All trainees start with a baseline of 65%. As you clear each weak area with a retest, this score climbs up to 100%, showing you are fully prepared and exam-ready.

Recovery Bonus Points (Elo Rating)

Bonus points added to your score when you fix your mistakes and pass retests.

What Does "Elo" Mean? (Full Form & Origin)

Origin / Creator: The Elo system is named after its inventor, Dr. Arpad Elo (a physicist and mathematician who created the world-standard skill measurement method).

Full Form in Learning: In modern training platforms, it stands for Expected Learning Outcome (Relative Competency Rating).

Simple Explanation: Just like a chess rating, your Elo score is a single number showing your true skill level. Scoring well on hard tests increases your Elo; clearing weak areas gives you recovery points.

How the Recovery Bonus Works

This bonus rewards trainees who go back, review their weak areas, and correct their mistakes. Every time you pass a retest for a weak topic, you earn a flat +50 Elo points.

Total Recovery Bonus = Cleared Topics x 50 Elo pts

Sampling Design

Horvitz-Thompson Estimator & Weights

▸ Core Formula: Y_HT = ∑(y_i / π_i) where π_i is probability of selection.
▸ Multiplier Rule: Inverse sampling probabilities ensure design-unbiased estimates in NSS multi-stage frames.
Macroeconomics

GDP Deflator vs Consumer Price Index

▸ Coverage: GDP deflator measures all domestically produced goods; CPI measures fixed household consumption basket.
▸ Index Type: GDP Deflator is a Paasche-like implicit index, while CPI uses Laspeyres modified formula.

Sampling Design

Horvitz-Thompson Estimator & Multipliers

▸ Unbiasedness: E[Y_HT] = Y is guaranteed regardless of sampling structure.
▸ Variance: Sen-Yates-Grundy variance formulation applies when sample size is fixed.
National Accounts

Supply and Use Tables (SUT) Balance

▸ Accounting Identity: Total Supply at Purchasers' Prices = Total Use at Purchasers' Prices.
▸ Base Year: 2011-12 benchmark tables provide structural weights for GDP deflators.
CAPI Protocol

GPS Verification & Non-Response Imputation

▸ Validation: GPS coordinates must match census enumeration block within 50-meter tolerance.
▸ Imputation: Hot-deck donor imputation is applied for item non-response.
Price Index Numbers

CPI Basket Splicing & Substitution Bias

▸ Laspeyres Bias: Fixed base quantities overestimate inflation due to consumer price substitution.
▸ Splicing Method: Ratio of indices during overlapping base year links old and rebased series.
Data Governance

DPDP Act 2023 Microdata Anonymization

▸ K-Anonymity (k ≥ 5): Every quasi-identifier combination must share at least 5 respondent records.
▸ Suppression: Cell counts < 3 in small district aggregates must be suppressed or perturbed.
Labour Statistics

PLFS Usual vs Current Weekly Activity Status

▸ Usual Status (ps+ss): Activity pursued for a relatively long time during reference 365 days.
▸ CWS Status: Activity status determined with reference period of last 7 preceding days.

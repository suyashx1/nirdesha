Architectural Pillars

Core Modules of the Nirdesha Framework

Built upon transparent evaluation standards, data integrity, and evidence-weighted guidance protocols.

Competency Engine

Maintains standardized competency taxonomies across administrative, statistical, survey methodology, and data analysis cadres. Identifies precise gaps between current capabilities and departmental requirements.

Explore Taxonomy Standards →

Dual-Metric Rating System

Maintains clear separation between Skill Confidence Score (0-100 evidence-weighted) and Competitive Assessment Rating (Elo-style update formula with decay), guaranteeing impartial proficiency records.

Read Rating Specification →

Personalized Guidance

Delivers dynamic training recommendations mapped to NSSTA and official training curriculums, preventing redundant instruction and accelerating critical capability development.
